Rob Case, new Chairman of MycoTechnology Board MycoTechnology, Inc. has announced Rob Case, member of the MycoTechnology Board of Directors, as its new Chairman of the Board. He succeeds Co-Founder and former Chairman, Alan Hahn, who will continue to support the company as a member of the board.
